When You May Need Chimney Sweep Help

Chimney sweeping is a routine part of fireplace maintenance. Homeowners often request sweep help in these situations:

Common reasons to request a sweep

  • Annual pre-season or post-season maintenance
  • Visible soot or debris around the firebox
  • Smoky smell when the fireplace isn't in use
  • Reduced draft or smoke entering the room
  • Before using a fireplace for the first time
  • After an extended period of non-use

What to know before requesting help

  • Sweeping frequency depends on usage and fuel type
  • Wood-burning fireplaces typically benefit from annual sweeps
  • Gas fireplaces may have different maintenance needs
  • A sweep may identify other issues like liner damage
  • Costs vary based on chimney condition and access
  • Requests are reviewed — we do not guarantee placement
