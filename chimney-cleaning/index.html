When You May Need Chimney Cleaning Help

Regular chimney cleaning is an important part of maintaining a safe and functional fireplace. Homeowners in the Hudson Valley often request cleaning help in these situations:

Common reasons to request cleaning

  • Before or after heating season
  • Soot or debris visible in the firebox
  • Smoky smell when fireplace is not in use
  • More than a year since last cleaning
  • After purchasing a home with a fireplace
  • Fireplace used frequently throughout winter

What to know before requesting help

  • Cleaning frequency depends on use and fuel type
  • Creosote buildup is a common concern in wood-burning fireplaces
  • A cleaning may reveal liner or structural issues
  • Gas fireplace cleaning differs from wood-burning cleaning
  • Costs vary based on chimney condition and access
  • Requests are reviewed — we do not guarantee placement
