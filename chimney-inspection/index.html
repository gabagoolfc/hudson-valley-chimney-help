When You May Need a Chimney Inspection

A chimney inspection can provide a clearer picture of your chimney's condition. Homeowners often request inspection help in these situations:

Common reasons to request an inspection

  • Before using a fireplace for the first time
  • When purchasing a home with a fireplace
  • After noticing odors, smoke, or draft issues
  • After storm or weather-related damage
  • When visible cracks or damage are present
  • If the chimney hasn't been checked in several years

What to know before requesting help

  • Inspection levels vary — basic visual to camera inspection
  • An inspection can identify issues before they worsen
  • Results may recommend cleaning or repairs
  • Home buyers often request inspections before closing
  • Costs vary based on inspection type and chimney condition
  • Requests are reviewed — we do not guarantee placement
