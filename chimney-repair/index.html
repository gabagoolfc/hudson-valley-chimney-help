When You May Need Chimney Repair Help

Chimneys are exposed to weather year-round, and structural issues can develop over time. Homeowners often request chimney repair help when they notice:

Common reasons to request repair help

  • Water stains near the fireplace or on ceilings
  • Cracked or crumbling mortar between bricks
  • Missing, damaged, or rusted chimney cap
  • Flashing issues around the chimney base
  • Spalling or cracked bricks on the chimney exterior
  • Chimney leaning or structural concerns

What to know before requesting help

  • Repair needs vary widely — an inspection often comes first
  • Water intrusion can cause significant long-term damage
  • Tuckpointing addresses mortar deterioration between bricks
  • Chimney cap issues can allow water and animals inside
  • Liner concerns are often identified during repair assessment
  • Costs vary based on extent of damage and access
