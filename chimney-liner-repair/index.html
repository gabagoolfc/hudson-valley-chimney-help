When You May Need Chimney Liner Repair Help

Chimney liners protect the chimney structure and help direct combustion gases safely out of the home. Liner issues are often identified during an inspection. Homeowners request liner repair help when:

Common reasons to request liner help

  • Inspection revealed cracked or damaged flue tiles
  • Smoke or odors entering the home from the fireplace
  • Older home with an unlined or aging clay tile liner
  • Converting fireplace to a gas insert or different fuel type
  • Following chimney fire or extreme temperature damage
  • Visible deterioration in the firebox area

What to know before requesting help

  • Liner condition is typically assessed via camera inspection
  • Repair options vary: relining, patching, or full replacement
  • Metal liners are common for gas appliances
  • Clay tile liners are common in older masonry chimneys
  • Costs vary based on liner type, length, and access
  • Requests are reviewed — we do not guarantee placement
